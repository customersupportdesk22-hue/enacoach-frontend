How to book

  1. Tap Book Mombasa to Nairobi and choose your travel date.
  2. Select a bus time, your boarding and dropping stops and your seat.
  3. Enter passenger details and tap Proceed to Payment.
  4. Pay with M-Pesa in the chat. Your ticket arrives there once payment is confirmed.
